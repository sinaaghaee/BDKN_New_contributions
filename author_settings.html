The released D’Angelo scalar code has a network-count factor in its driving gradient; the JAX FoRDE adapter does not. Functional KDE-WGD also changes the prior representation. A comparison across these methods therefore changes more than the repulsion term. The FoRDE matched control preserves its adapter and removes only repulsion. BDKN and the FoRDE regression combination are our extensions.
